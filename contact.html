FAQ

“Why is sustainability important?”

Sustainability is important because it helps protect natural resources, reduce pollution and support future generations.

“What are environmental regulations?”

Environmental regulations are rules that are designed to protect the environment and public health.

“How can businesses be more sustainable?”

Businesses can reduce waste, save energy and improve environmentally friendly practices.

“What is an environmental assessment?”

An environmental assessment identifies how staff can affect the environment.
